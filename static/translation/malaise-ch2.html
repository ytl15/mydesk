01相对主义与自我实现§1–5

我们可以从近来在美国出版的一本影响极大的书切入：阿兰·布鲁姆的《美国精神的封闭》。这本书本身就是一个颇为瞩目的现象。一位学院派政治理论家写了一本讨论当代学生思想氛围的著作，竟连续数月位列《纽约时报》畅销书榜，连作者本人也大感意外。它触动了人们心中的某根弦。

英 13
We can pick it up through a very influential recent book in the United States, Allan Bloom’s The Closing of the American Mind. The book itself was a rather remarkable phenomenon: a work by an academic political theorist about the climate of opinion among today’s students, it held a place on the New York Times best-seller list for several months, greatly to the surprise of the author. It touched a chord.

这本书对当今受过教育的青年持严厉的批判态度。在布鲁姆看来，他们人生观中最突出的特征，是接受了一种颇为轻率的相对主义：每个人都有自己的“价值观”，而这些价值观是无法争辩的。但正如布鲁姆所指出的，这不只是一种认识论立场，即一种关于理性所能确立之界限的看法；它同时也被当作一种道德立场：我们不应质疑他人的价值观。那是他自己的事，是他对生活的选择，理应受到尊重。因此，这种相对主义有一部分建立在相互尊重的原则之上。

英 14
The stance it took was severely critical of today’s educated youth. The main feature it noted in their outlook on life was their acceptance of a rather facile relativism. Everybody has his or her own “values,” and about these it is impossible to argue. But as Bloom noted, this was not just an epistemological position, a view about the limits of what reason can establish; it was also held as a moral position: one ought not to challenge another’s values. That is their concern, their life choice, and it ought to be respected. The relativism was partly grounded in a principle of mutual respect.

换句话说，这种相对主义本身就是某种个人主义的衍生形式，其原则大致如下：每个人都有权按照自己对什么真正重要、什么具有价值的理解，发展自己的生活方式。人们受到召唤，要忠于自己，追求自己的自我实现。至于自我实现究竟包含什么内容，归根结底必须由每个人自行决定；任何其他人都不能、也不应该试图替他规定。

英 14
In other words, the relativism was itself an offshoot of a form of individualism, whose principle is something like this: everyone has a right to develop their own form of life, grounded on their own sense of what is really important or of value. People are called upon to be true to themselves and to seek their own self-fulfilment. What this consists of, each must, in the last instance, determine for him- or herself. No one else can or should try to dictate its content.

这种立场在今天已经耳熟能详。它体现了我们可以称为“自我实现的个人主义”的东西。这种个人主义广泛流行于当代，并且自20世纪60年代以来，在西方社会变得格外强盛。其他一些有影响力的著作也抓住并讨论了这一现象，例如丹尼尔·贝尔的《资本主义文化矛盾》、克里斯托弗·拉西的《自恋文化》和《最小的自我》，以及吉尔·利波维茨基的《空虚时代》。

英 14
This is a familiar enough position today. It reflects what we could call the individualism of self-fulfilment, which is widespread in our times and has grown particularly strong in Western societies since the 1960s. It has been picked up on and discussed in other influential books: Daniel Bell’s The Cultural Contradictions of Capitalism, Christopher Lasch’s The Culture of Narcissism and The Minimal Self, and Gilles Lipovetsky’s L’ère du vide.

这些著作中都能听见忧虑的声音，只是在利波维茨基那里或许没有那么明显。这种忧虑大体沿着我先前在第一重隐忧下勾勒的思路展开：这种个人主义意味着以自我为中心，并随之将那些超越自我的重大问题和关切拒之门外，甚至对它们浑然不觉——无论这些问题和关切属于宗教、政治还是历史。结果，生活变得狭窄而扁平。11 而且，这种忧虑往往还会延伸到我所说的第三个领域：这些作者担心，这一文化转向可能造成极为严重的政治后果。

英 15
The tone of concern is audible in all these, although perhaps less marked in Lipovetsky. It runs roughly along the lines I outlined above under theme 1. This individualism involves a centring on the self and a concomitant shutting out, or even unawareness, of the greater issues or concerns that transcend the self, be they religious, political, historical. As a consequence, life is narrowed or flattened.11 And the worry characteristically spills over into the third area I described: these authors are concerned about the possibly dire political consequences of this shift in the culture.
02批判遗漏的道德理想§6–10

这些作者对当代文化的严厉批评，有许多地方我是赞同的。下文很快就会说明，我认为今天受到广泛拥护的相对主义是一个深刻的错误，在某些方面甚至会自我挫败。自我实现的文化似乎确实使许多人看不见那些超越自身的关切；它采取了庸俗化和自我放纵的形式，这一点似乎也显而易见。这甚至可能造成某种荒诞：一群努力“成为自己”的人之间出现了新的趋同模式；更有甚者，自我认同不稳的人又转向各式自封的专家和导师，从而形成新的依赖，而这些专家和导师往往顶着科学权威或某种异域灵性的光环。

英 15
Now there is much that I agree with in the strictures these writers make of contemporary culture. As I shall explain in a minute, I think the relativism widely espoused today is a profound mistake, even in some respects self-stultifying. It seems true that the culture of self-fulfilment has led many people to lose sight of concerns that transcend them. And it seems obvious that it has taken trivialized and self-indulgent forms. This can even result in a sort of absurdity, as new modes of conformity arise among people who are striving to be themselves, and beyond this, new forms of dependence, as people insecure in their identities turn to all sorts of self-appointed experts and guides, shrouded with the prestige of science or some exotic spirituality.

不过，这些作者论证的总体倾向中，仍有一点是我无法认同的。在布鲁姆那里，这一点表现得最清楚，尤其鲜明地体现在他对所描述文化的轻蔑语气中。他似乎没有看到，这里仍有一种强有力的道德理想在发挥作用，无论这种理想的表现形式多么低劣、多么扭曲。自我实现背后的道德理想，就是以一种特有的现代含义来理解的“忠于自己”。二十多年前，莱昂内尔·特里林在一部影响深远的著作中出色地界定了这个理想：他概括了它的现代形式，并将其同早先的形式区别开来。这一区别就体现在书名《诚与真》（*Sincerity and Authenticity*）中。沿用他的说法，我将把这一当代理想称为“本真性”。

英 16
But there is something I nevertheless want to resist in the thrust of the arguments that these authors present. It emerges clearest in Bloom, perhaps most strongly in his tone of contempt for the culture he is describing. He doesn’t seem to recognize that there is a powerful moral ideal at work here, however debased and travestied its expression might be. The moral ideal behind self-fulfilment is that of being true to oneself, in a specifically modern understanding of that term. A couple of decades ago, this was brilliantly defined by Lionel Trilling in an influential book, in which he encapsulated that modern form and distinguished it from earlier ones. The distinction is expressed in the title of the book, Sincerity and Authenticity, and following him I am going to use the term “authenticity” for the contemporary ideal.

所谓道德理想，我指的是一幅关于何为更好或更高生活方式的图景。在这里，“更好”和“更高”并不是根据我们碰巧想要什么或需要什么来界定的；这幅图景为我们应当欲求什么提供了标准。

英 16
What do I mean by a moral ideal? I mean a picture of what a better or higher mode of life would be, where “better” and “higher” are defined not in terms of what we happen to desire or need, but offer a standard of what we ought to desire.

“自恋”（拉西的用语）或“享乐主义”（贝尔的描述）这类说法，其言外之意是，这里根本没有道德理想在发挥作用；即使表面上存在某种理想，也应该把它看成掩饰自我放纵的幌子。正如布鲁姆所说：“绝大多数学生虽然和任何人一样，都希望能把自己看得不错，却也知道自己正忙于个人事业和人际关系。有一套自我实现的说辞，给这种生活镀上了一层迷人的光泽，但他们看得出，这种生活并没有什么特别高尚之处。生存主义已经取代英雄主义，成为受人推崇的品质。”12 我毫不怀疑，这段话刻画了某些人，甚至可能是许多人；但若以为它能使我们洞察这场文化变迁，洞察这一道德理想的力量，那就大错特错了。我们必须理解这种力量，才可能解释为什么就连自我放纵者也会把这一理想用作一层虚伪的“镀层”。

英 16
The force of terms like “narcissism” (Lasch’s word), or “hedonism” (Bell’s description), is to imply that there is no moral ideal at work here; or if there is, on the surface, that it should rather be seen as a screen for self-indulgence. As Bloom puts it, “The great majority of students, although they as much as anyone want to think well of themselves, are aware that they are busy with their own careers and their relationships. There is a certain rhetoric of self-fulfilment that gives a patina of glamor to this life, but they can see that there is nothing particularly noble about it. Survivalism has taken the place of heroism as the admired quality.”12I have no doubt that this describes some, perhaps lots, of people, but it is a big mistake to think that it allows us insight into the change in our culture, into the power of this moral ideal — which we need to understand if we are to explain even why it is used as a hypocritical “patina” by the self-indulgent.

我们在这里需要理解的，正是“自我实现”等观念背后的道德力量。一旦试图把它简单解释成某种利己主义或道德松弛，解释成相较于一个更严厉、要求更高的旧时代而言的自我放纵，我们就已经偏离了正轨。“放任”的说法也错失了这一点。道德松弛当然存在，而且并非我们这个时代所独有。我们需要解释的，是我们这个时代特有的东西。问题不只在于人们为了追求事业而牺牲爱情关系和对子女的照料；类似的事情或许向来都有。关键在于，今天许多人觉得自己受到了召唤，要这样做，觉得自己应该如此；如果不这样做，他们便会觉得自己的人生仿佛虚度了，无法得到实现。

英 17
What we need to understand here is the moral force behind notions like self-fulfilment. Once we try to explain this simply as a kind of egoism, or a species of moral laxism, a self-indulgence with regard to a tougher, more exigent earlier age, we are already off the track. Talk of “permissiveness” misses this point. Moral laxity there is, and our age is not alone in this. What we need to explain is what is peculiar to our time. It’s not just that people sacrifice their love relationships, and the care of their children, to pursue their careers. Something like this has perhaps always existed. The point is that today many people feel called to do this, feel they ought to do this, feel their lives would be somehow wasted or unfulfilled if they didn’t do it.
03本真性文化的失语§11–15

因此，这类批判所忽略的，正是本真性理想的道德力量。这个理想似乎连同它的当代形式一道，就这样在无形中受到了贬低。如果我们能够转向对立的一方，从那里得到对这个理想的辩护，问题倒还不算严重；但在这里，我们同样会感到失望。人们对本真性的拥护采取了某种柔性相对主义的形式，这意味着，对任何道德理想作有力辩护似乎都成了禁区。因为，正如我刚才所说，这一理想意味着某些生活方式确实高于另一些；而对个人自我实现持宽容态度的文化，却回避这样的主张。正如人们经常指出的，这使他们的立场显得自相矛盾，并且会自我挫败，因为相对主义本身至少在一定程度上也是由一种道德理想推动的。但无论是否自洽，人们通常采取的就是这种立场。本真性理想沉降到了公理的层面：人们不去质疑它，却也从不加以阐明。

英 17
Thus what gets lost in this critique is the moral force of the ideal of authenticity. It is somehow being implicitly discredited, along with its contemporary forms. That would not be so bad if we could turn to the opposition for a defence. But here we will be disappointed. That the espousal of authenticity takes the form of a kind of soft relativism means that the vigorous defence of any moral ideal is somehow off limits. For the implications, as I have just described them above, are that some forms of life are indeed higher than others, and the culture of tolerance for individual self-fulfilment shies away from these claims. This means, as has often been pointed out, that there is something contradictory and self-defeating in their position, since the relativism itself is powered (at least partly) by a moral ideal. But consistently or not, this is the position usually adopted. The ideal sinks to the level of an axiom, something one doesn’t challenge but also never expounds.

身处我所谓“本真性文化”中的人们，在接受这一理想的同时，也支持某种得到许多其他人拥护的自由主义。这就是中立自由主义。它的一项基本信条是：自由社会必须在何为美好生活的问题上保持中立。美好生活，是每个人以自己的方式所追求的生活；政府若在这个问题上选边站，就会有失公允，因而也无法做到平等尊重所有公民。13 尽管这一学派的许多作者——德沃金和金里卡也在其中——都是柔性相对主义的坚定反对者，他们的理论最终仍把有关美好生活的讨论放逐到了政治辩论的边缘。

英 18
In adopting the ideal, people in the culture of authenticity, as I want to call it, give support to a certain kind of liberalism, which has been espoused by many others as well. This is the liberalism of neutrality. One of its basic tenets is that a liberal society must be neutral on questions of what constitutes a good life. The good life is what each individual seeks, in his or her own way, and government would be lacking in impartiality, and thus in equal respect for all citizens, if it took sides on this question.13 Although many of the writers in this school are passionate opponents of soft relativism (Dworkin and Kymlicka among them), the result of their theory is to banish discussions about the good life to the margins of political debate.

结果，现代文化的一项构成性理想，竟变得异常难以言明。14 它的反对者轻视它，它的拥护者却无法把它说清楚。整场争论仿佛合谋把它推入阴影，使它隐而不见。这会造成有害的后果。不过，在继续讨论这些后果之前，我想先指出另外两个同样合力加深这种沉默的因素。

英 18
The result is an extraordinary inarticulacy about one of the constitutive ideals of modern culture.14 Its opponents slight it, and its friends can’t speak of it. The whole debate conspires to put it in the shade, to render it invisible. This has detrimental consequences. But before going on to these, I want to mention two other factors that conspire to intensify this silence.

其中之一，是道德主观主义在我们文化中的支配力。所谓道德主观主义，我指的是这样一种观点：道德立场丝毫不以理性或事物的本性为根据；归根结底，我们每个人之所以采取某种立场，只是因为发现自己受到了它的吸引。按照这种观点，理性无法裁决道德争端。当然，你可以向某个人指出，他的立场可能造成某些他未曾想到的后果。因此，本真性的批评者可以指出，每个人都追求自我实现可能带来怎样的社会和政治后果。但如果你的对话者仍然愿意坚持原有立场，你便再也说不出什么来反驳他。

英 18
One of them is the hold of moral subjectivism in our culture. By this I mean the view that moral positions are not in any way grounded in reason or the nature of things but are ultimately just adopted by each of us because we find ourselves drawn to them. On this view, reason can’t adjudicate moral disputes. Of course, you can point out to someone certain consequences of his position he may not have thought about. So the critics of authenticity can point to the possible social and political results of each person seeking self-fulfilment. But if your interlocutor still feels like holding to his original position, nothing further can be said to gainsay him.

这种观点的根据很复杂，远远不止柔性相对主义的道德理由，尽管主观主义显然为这种相对主义提供了重要支撑。显然，置身于当代本真性文化的许多人乐于接受这种关于理性之作用——或无作用——的理解。或许更出人意料的是，许多反对他们的人也接受这种理解，并因此越发不指望能够改变当代文化。如果年轻人真的不关心任何超越自我的事业，我们还能对他们说些什么呢？

英 19
The grounds for this view are complex and go far beyond the moral reasons for soft relativism, although subjectivism clearly provides an important backing for this relativism. Obviously, lots of people into the contemporary culture of authenticity are happy to espouse this understanding of the role (or non-role) of reason. What is perhaps more surprising, so are a great many of their opponents, who therefore are led to despair all the more of reforming contemporary culture. If the youth really don’t care for causes that transcend the self, then what can you say to them?
04主观主义与社会科学解释§16–21

当然，也有批评者坚持认为，存在可以诉诸理性的标准。15 他们相信确有人性可言，而且对人性的理解能够表明：某些生活方式是正确的，另一些是错误的；某些生活方式高于并优于另一些。这一立场的哲学根源可以追溯到亚里士多德。相比之下，现代主观主义者往往强烈批评亚里士多德，指责他的“形而上学生物学”已经过时，在今天完全不足以令人信服。

英 19
Of course, there are critics who hold that there are standards in reason.15 They think that there is such a thing as human nature, and that an understanding of this will show certain ways of life to be right and others wrong, certain ways to be higher and better than others. The philosophical roots of this position are in Aristotle. By contrast, modern subjectivists tend to be very critical of Aristotle, and complain that his “metaphysical biology” is out of date and thoroughly unbelievable today.

然而，持这种观点的哲学家通常反对本真性理想；在他们看来，这一理想错误地背离了那些根植于人性的标准。他们自然没有理由去阐明这一理想究竟意味着什么；而拥护这一理想的人，又常常因自己的主观主义观点而失去了阐明它的意愿。

英 19
But philosophers who think like this have generally been opponents of the ideal of authenticity; they have seen it as part of a mistaken departure from the standards rooted in human nature. They had no reason to articulate what it is about; while those who upheld it have been frequently discouraged from doing so by their subjectivist views.

遮蔽本真性作为道德理想之重要性的第三个因素，是社会科学惯常采用的解释方式。这类解释通常回避诉诸道德理想，转而求助于据称更坚实、更切近现实的因素。因此，我在这里一直关注的两项现代性特征——个人主义和工具理性的扩张——常被解释为社会变迁的副产品，例如工业化、流动性增强或城市化的派生结果。这里当然存在需要追踪的重要因果关系；但诉诸这些关系的解释，往往完全绕开了一个问题：文化和观念上的这些变化，是否也有一部分源自相关道德理想自身的内在力量？其隐含的回答往往是否定的。16

英 20
A third factor that has obscured the importance of authenticity as a moral ideal has been the normal fashion of social science explanation. This has generally shied away from invoking moral ideals and has tended to have recourse to supposedly harder and more down to earth factors in its explanation. And so the features of modernity I have been focussing on here, individualism and the expansion of instrumental reason, have often been accounted for as by-products of social change: for instance, as spin-offs from industrialization, or greater mobility, or urbanization. There are certainly important causal relations to be traced here, but the accounts that invoke them frequently skirt altogether the issue whether these changes in culture and outlook owe anything to their own inherent power as moral ideals. The implicit answer is often negative.16

当然，那些据说催生了新观念的社会变化，本身也需要得到解释；而这种解释必然要在一定程度上诉诸人的动机，除非我们以为，工业化和城市发展完全是人们在梦游中推动的。我们需要知道，是什么推动人们持续把社会引向某个方向，例如把更多技术用于生产，或促成人口更大规模地集中。然而，人们经常援引的是非道德性动机。我所说的，是即使与任何道德理想毫无关联，也能够驱使人们行动的动机——这里的道德理想沿用我先前的定义。因此，我们经常看到，这些社会变化被解释为人们渴望获得更多财富、权力、生存手段或支配他人的手段。这些欲求固然都可以被编织进道德理想，却并非必然如此；因此，以它们为根据的解释便被认为足够“坚实”、足够“科学”。

英 20
Of course, the social changes that are supposed to spawn the new outlook must themselves be explained, and this will involve some recourse to human motivations, unless we suppose that industrialization or the growth of cities occurred entirely in a fit of absence of mind. We need some notion of what moved people to push steadily in one direction, for example towards the greater application of technology to production, or towards greater concentrations of population. But what are often invoked are motivations that are non-moral. By that I mean motivations that can actuate people quite without connection to any moral ideal, as I defined this earlier. So we very often find these social changes explained in terms of the desire for greater wealth, or power, or the means of survival or control over others. Though all these things can be woven into moral ideals, they need not be, and so explanation in terms of them is considered sufficiently “hard” and “scientific.”

即使个人自由和工具理性的扩张被认为具有内在吸引力，而这种内在吸引力能够帮助解释它们的兴起，人们也常常从非道德的意义上理解这种吸引力。换言之，人们不从道德角度来理解这些观念的影响力，而只是看到它们似乎能给人带来好处——不论这个人的道德观如何，甚至不论他是否拥有道德观。自由让你做自己想做的事；工具理性得到更广泛的运用，就能让你获得更多自己想要的东西，无论那是什么。17

英 21
Even where individual freedom and the enlargement of instrumental reason are seen as ideas whose intrinsic attractions can help explain their rise, this attraction is frequently understood in non-moral terms. That is, the power of these ideas is often understood not in terms of their moral force but just because of the advantages they seem to bestow on people regardless of their moral outlook, or even whether they have a moral outlook. Freedom allows you to do what you want, and the greater application of instrumental reason gets you more of what you want, whatever that is.17

这一切加深了本真性这一道德理想周围的幽暗。当代文化的批评者贬低它作为理想的地位，甚至把它同一种非道德欲望混为一谈，即不受干涉地为所欲为。本真性文化的捍卫者则被自身立场推入失语。哲学世界中主观主义的普遍影响，加上中立自由主义的力量，进一步强化了这样的感觉：这些问题既无法讨论，也不应讨论。更有甚者，社会科学似乎还在告诉我们：要理解当代本真性文化这样的现象，不应在解释中诉诸道德理想，而应该从生产方式近来的变化、18 青年消费的新模式或富裕生活带来的安全感等方面来理解这一切。

英 21
The result of all this has been to thicken the darkness around the moral ideal of authenticity. Critics of contemporary culture tend to disparage it as an ideal, even to confound it with a non-moral desire to do what one wants without interference. The defenders of this culture are pushed into inarticulacy about it by their own outlook. The general force of subjectivism in our philosophical world and the power of neutral liberalism intensify the sense that these issues can’t and shouldn’t be talked about. And then on top of it all, social science seems to be telling us that to understand such phenomena as the contemporary culture of authenticity, we shouldn’t have recourse in our explanations to such things as moral ideals but should see it all in terms of, say, recent changes in the mode of production,18 or new patterns of youth consumption, or the security of affluence.
05重新寻回本真性理想§22–27

这要紧吗？我认为非常要紧。当代文化批评者所攻击的许多现象，其实都是这一理想的低劣而偏离正轨的形态。也就是说，它们从这一理想中生发出来，其践行者也援引这一理想，却并没有真正——或者说“本真地”（！）——实现它。柔性相对主义就是一个典型例子。布鲁姆看到了它的道德基础：“真理的相对性不是一种理论洞见，而是一项道德公设，是自由社会的条件——至少［学生们］如此认为。”19 但我要主张，它实际上歪曲并最终背叛了这一道德洞见。柔性相对主义不仅不能成为拒绝本真性理想的理由，反而应当以这一理想的名义遭到拒绝。这正是我要论证的。

英 22
Does this matter? I think so, very much. Many of the things critics of contemporary culture attack are debased and deviant forms of this ideal. That is, they flow from it, and their practitioners invoke it, but in fact they don’t represent an authentic (!) fulfilment of it. Soft relativism is a case in point. Bloom sees that it has a moral basis: “The relativity of truth is not a theoretical insight but a moral postulate, the condition of a free society, or so [the students] see it.”19 But in fact, I would like to claim, it travesties and eventually betrays this moral insight. So far from being a reason to reject the moral ideal of authenticity, it should itself be rejected in its name. Or so I would like to argue.

同样的批评也适用于借本真性之名漠视一切超越自我的事物：把我们的过去斥为无关紧要，否认公民身份的要求、团结互助的义务或自然环境的需求。同样，以本真性为名，把人际关系仅仅看作个人自我实现的工具，也应当被视作一种会自我挫败的歪曲。把选择能力本身肯定为一种应当最大化的善，则是这一理想偏离正轨的副产品。

英 22
A similar point can be made for those appeals to authenticity that justify ignoring whatever transcends the self: for rejecting our past as irrelevant, or denying the demands of citizenship, or the duties of solidarity, or the needs of the natural environment. Similarly, justifying in the name of authenticity a concept of relationships as instrumental to individual self-fulfilment should also be seen as a self-stultifying travesty. The affirmation of the power of choice as itself a good to be maximized is a deviant product of the ideal.

如果事情大体如此，那么能够把这一点说清楚就十分重要。因为这样一来，我们便能有理有据地对那些把自己的生命投入这些偏离形态的人说些什么，并可能真正影响他们的生活；其中一些话，他们或许会听进去。把这一理想清楚阐明出来，在这里具有道德意义：它不仅能够纠正可能错误的看法，还能让人们更真切、更鲜明地感受到那个已然指导着他们生活的理想的力量；而当理想变得鲜明，人们便更有能力以更充分、更完整的方式践行它。

英 22
Now if something like this is true, then it matters to be able to say it. For then one has something to say, in all reason, to the people who invest their lives in these deviant forms. And this may make a difference to their lives. Some of these things may be heard. Articulacy here has a moral point, not just in correcting what may be wrong views but also in making the force of an ideal that people are already living by more palpable, more vivid for them; and by making it more vivid, empowering them to live up to it in a fuller and more integral fashion.

我所提出的立场，既不同于当代文化的鼓吹者，也不同于它的抨击者。我不像鼓吹者那样，认为这种文化中的一切都处在应有的状态；在这一点上，我倾向于赞同抨击者。但与他们不同，我认为应当认真对待本真性，把它视为一种道德理想。我也不同于各种中间立场：后者认为，这种文化带来了一些好处，例如个人获得了更多自由，但这些好处也伴随着某些危险，例如公民意识的削弱；因此，最好的办法是在收益与代价之间找到理想的平衡点。

英 23
What I am suggesting is a position distinct from both boosters and knockers of contemporary culture. Unlike the boosters, I do not believe that everything is as it should be in this culture. Here I tend to agree with the knockers. But unlike them, I think that authenticity should be taken seriously as a moral ideal. I differ also from the various middle positions, which hold that there are some good things in this culture (like greater freedom for the individual), but that these come at the expense of certain dangers (like a weakening of the sense of citizenship), so that one’s best policy is to find the ideal point of trade-off between advantages and costs.

我所给出的，其实是另一幅图景：这是一个已经退化、但本身极有价值的理想；我甚至愿意说，现代人无法将它彻底否弃。因此，我们需要的既不是彻底的谴责，也不是不加批判的赞美，更不是经过精心权衡的折中。我们需要做的，是重新寻回这一理想；通过这项工作，这一理想反过来能够帮助我们修复自己的实践。

英 23
The picture I am offering is rather that of an ideal that has degraded but that is very worthwhile in itself, and indeed, I would like to say, unrepudiable by moderns. So what we need is neither root-and-branch condemnation nor uncritical praise; and not a carefully balanced trade-off. What we need is a work of retrieval, through which this ideal can help us restore our practice.

要接受这一主张，就必须相信三件颇具争议的事：（1）本真性是一项正当的理想；（2）我们能够运用理性对理想展开论证，并判断实践是否符合这些理想；（3）这类论证能够产生实际影响。第一项信念逆着本真性文化批评的主流而行；第二项要求拒绝主观主义；第三项则同那些现代性理论不相容——后者认为，无论“体制”被界定为资本主义、工业社会还是官僚制度，我们都已被它禁锢在现代文化之中。接下来，我希望能使其中一些主张显得可信。让我们先从这个理想本身谈起。

英 23
To go along with this, you have to believe three things, all controversial: (1) that authenticity is a valid ideal; (2) that you can argue in reason about ideals and about the conformity of practices to these ideals; and (3) that these arguments can make a difference. The first belief flies in the face of the major thrust of criticism of the culture of authenticity, the second involves rejecting subjectivism, and the third is incompatible with those accounts of modernity that see us as imprisoned in modern culture by the “system,” whether this is defined as capitalism, industrial society, or bureaucracy. I hope to be able to make some of this plausible in what follows. Let me start with the ideal.
