2「教皇」这张卡带领我们来到了「赐福行为」面前。当我们试图解读整张卡的结构以及每一个元素时，必须记住上述这句话。永远不要忽略这张卡涉及的是赐福及相关事宜教皇、跪在他面前的侍祭、身后的两根柱子，他的冠冕与手中握著的三重十字法杖。然而赐福究竟是什么？来源和作用又是什么？谁拥有赐福的权力？这在人的灵性生活中又扮演著什么角色？
中 159–160 · 英 98
2The Card “The Pope” puts us in the presence of the act of benediction. It is essential to have this in mind when one undertakes the interpretation not only of the structure of the whole Card but also of each of its particular elements. One should therefore never lose from sight that in the first place it is a matter of benediction and everything associated with it—no matter who the Pope may be or who the acolytes kneeling before him are, and no matter what the two columns behind the Pope signify, and no matter what his tiara and the triple cross he is holding symbolise. What is benediction? What is its source and its effect? Who has the authority to bestow benediction? What role does it play in the spiritual life of humanity?
3赐福不是一个简单的为他人许善愿的举动，也不是把个人的想法和意志加诸他人身上的魔法。它可以引发神力，令受福者与赐福者都能超越个人的想法和意志。换言之，就本质而言，这是神父的职责。

3Now, benediction is more than a simple good wish made for others; it is also more than a magical impress of personal thought and will upon others. It is the putting into action of divine power transcending the individual thought and will of the one who is blessed as well as the one who is pronouncing the blessing. In other words it is an essentially sacerdotal act.
4卡巴拉将祈祷与赐福比喻成一种双向作用（上升与下降），类似血液循环。人的祈祷上升至神的面前，被神性「氧化」而转成至福，然后从上界降临至下界。这就是为什么卡上的那位侍祭的左手是向上举起的，另一位侍祭的右手则是向下压低的。教皇身后的两根蓝色柱子所象征的，就是这种双向上升与下降的祈祷与赐福。同时，教皇的左手举著一根十字朝上的三重法杖，它的位置是在「祈祷之柱」与祈祷中的侍祭这边。教皇的右手—在「赐福之柱」和正在接受（或鼓舞）这场仪式的侍祭那边—则做著赐福的手势。

4The Cabbala compares the role of prayer and benediction to a double movement, ascending and descending, similar to the circulation of the blood. The prayers of humanity rise towards God and, after having been divinely “oxidised”, are transformed into benedictions which descend below from above. This is why one of the acolytes of the Card has his left hand raised and the other has his right hand lowered. The two blue columns behind the Pope symbolise in the first place this twofold current—rising and descending—of prayers and benedictions. At the same time the Pope himself holds aloft a triple cross on the side with the “column of prayer” and the praying acolyte, whilst his right hand—on the side with the “column of benediction” and the acolyte receiving (or “inspiring”) benediction—makes the gesture of benediction.
5卡巴拉生命树图形的右侧与左侧分别是慈爱之柱与严峻之柱，它们和所罗门圣殿前的雅斤及波阿斯神柱（Jachin and Boaz），都代表著赐福与祈祷的行为，因为严峻能促成祈祷的力量，慈爱则能降福于人。波阿斯是一股上升的作用力，代表静脉的「蓝色血液」，雅斤则是下降的作用力，代表动脉含氧的「红色血液」。红色血液运送著含有生命力的至福氧气；蓝色血液则能清除「烈性」的碳酸机体。灵性生活的运作方式也是如此。不做任何祈祷的人会被灵性的窒息感所威胁；经常祈祷的人则会得到活力十足的至福感。因此，这两根柱子也象征著灵修上的实用价值——如同呼吸对有机体的意义一样。

5The two sides of the Cabbala—the “right” side and the “left” side—and the two columns of the Sephiroth Tree, the pillar of Mercy and that of Severity, and similarly the two pillars of the Temple of Solomon, Jachin and Boaz, correspond exactly to the two columns of prayer and benediction on this Card. Because it is Severity which stimulates prayer and it is Mercy which blesses. The venous “blue blood” of Boaz ascends and the arterial oxidised “red blood” of Jachin descends. The “red blood” bears the vivifying benediction of oxygen; the “blue blood” rids the organism of the “severity” of carbonic acid. It is the same in the spiritual life. Spiritual asphyxia menaces he who does not practise some form of prayer; he who practises it receives vivifying benediction in some form. The two columns therefore have an essentially practical significance—as practical spiritually as respiration is for the life of the organism.

6Thus, the first practical teaching of the fifth Arcanum—for the Major Arcana of the Tarot are spiritual exercises—relates to spiritual respiration.
7呼吸可以分为两种：水平呼吸是介于内外之间，垂直呼吸则介于上下界之间。死亡的创痛或临死前痛苦至极的感受，就是从水平呼吸急速转变成垂直呼吸所引发的。然而一个人生前如果已经学会垂直呼吸，便可免于承受「死亡的痛苦」。对他来说，呼吸的转换不再像直角一样尖锐，比较像是圆弧形的转化过程。这种转化过程不是突发的而是渐进的，是弧形的而不是直角的。

7There are two kinds of respiration: horizontal respiration which takes place between “outside” and “inside”, and vertical respiration which takes place between “above” and “below”. The “sting of death” or the essential crisis of the supreme agony is the abrupt passage from horizontal to vertical respiration. Yet he who has learnt vertical respiration whilst living will be spared from this “sting of death”. For him the passage from the one form of respiration to the other will not be of the nature of a right angle but rather the arc of a circle: . The transition will not be abrupt but gradual, and curved instead of rectangular.
8垂直呼吸就是祈祷与赐福或恩宠之间的转化作用。垂直呼吸的两种元素会在不同层次上心智、情绪和意志示现出来。因此，心中生起的问题若不是源自好奇或头脑的搜证癖，而是出自于对真相的渴望，基本上就算是一种祈祷。接著可能发生的是启蒙，也就是随祈祷而来的赐福或恩宠。同样地，真正的受难基本上也是一种祈祷。随之而至的抚慰、祥和与喜乐，则是祈祷获得的赐福或恩宠。

8Now, the essence of vertical respiration is the alternation between prayer and benediction or grace. These two elements of vertical respiration manifest themselves in all domains of the inner life—mind, heart and will. Thus a relevant problem for the mind, which is not due to curiosity or intellectual collectionism, but rather to the thirst for truth, is fundamentally a prayer. And the illumination by which it may be followed is the corresponding benediction or grace. True suffering, also, is fundamentally always a prayer. And the consolation, peace and joy which can follow are the effects of the benediction or grace corresponding to it.
9真正的、百分之百的努力，或是下了最真实的工夫，也是一种祈祷。当我们将努力用在智力工作时就是在祈请：愿人都尊你的名为圣。当我们贡献创意时，则是在祈请：愿你的国降临。当我们为人提供生活物资时，也等于是在祈请：我们日用的饮食，请今日赐给我们。一切以工作形式进行的祈祷，都有与之呼应的赐福或恩宠。

And all these forms of prayer in the language of work have their corresponding benedictions or graces.

21It is the sceptre of the Pope’s authority for this Card, just as the sphere formed from the double cup and topped by the cross is the sceptre of the Emperor. Just as the Emperor, guardian of the throne of David, represents towards heaven the human, i.e. the divine image and likeness in man, so does the Pope, guardian of the portal to the pillars of benediction and prayer, represent transcendental Divinity towards mankind. The two posts, that of Emperor and that of Pope, are two spiritual realities. They are as real as the head and the heart are in the life of the individual. The heart is the centre of respiration and blood circulation; the head is the centre of the nervous system and is the seat of thought.
22如同皇帝的职位不是众议会可以取代的——意即大卫的王冠不能被集体取代——教皇的职位或麦基洗德（撒冷王）的王冠也无法被基督信仰普世教会取代。不论西方玄学家预言的「天火」是否已经发射，不论神父的宝座是否依旧可见，还是已经被搁置在地下墓穴里，无论那些破坏性的预言说了什么，它仍然会在人类未来的历史中现身。

22And just as no parliament will replace the spiritual reality of the post of Emperor—the throne of David cannot be replaced by collectivity—so will no oecumenical council replace the spiritual reality of the post of Pope or the “throne of Melchizadek, king of plenitude (Salem)”. Whether the “cannon shot” predicted in esoteric circles of the Occident be given or not, whether the sacerdotal throne remains visible or whether it be installed in the catacombs, it will certainly remain present for ever in the future history of humanity—despite what the prophets of its destruction may say.
23历史就像人的生命一样不分昼夜地「运行」著。它有白天的一面，也有夜晚的一面。前者是外显的，后者是内隐的。夜晚的寂静与晦暗里充满著蓄势待发的事件，因此人类的无意识与超意识都属于「夜晚」的范畴。它代表著历史的魔法面向，躲在「白昼」后面隐匿地运作著。当福音书借由「日光」在地中海国家四处被宣扬时，「夜光」却造就了佛法的深层转变。原本追求个人解脱的涅槃思想被舍弃，取而代之的是解救苦难众生的慈悲行为。于是大乘佛法开始兴起，人们追求的精神理想有了辉煌的跃升，形成了代表亚洲道德价值的净土（天堂）思想。

23Because history—as, moreover, the life of the individual—is “worked” by day and by night. It has a diurnal aspect and a nocturnal aspect. The former is exoteric, whilst the latter is esoteric. The silence and obscurity of the night is always full of events in preparation—and all that which is unconscious or superconscious in the human being belongs to the domain of “night”. This is the magical side of history, the side of magical deeds and works acting behind the facade of history “by day”. Thus, when the Gospel was preached by the light of day in the countries around the Mediterranean, the nocturnal rays of the Gospel effected a profound transformation of Buddhism. There, the ideal of individual liberation by entering the state of nirvana gave way to the ideal of renouncing nirvana for the work of mercy towards suffering humanity. The ideal of mahayana, the great chariot, then had its resplendent ascent to the heaven of Asia’s moral values.
